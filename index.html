Where does Hibbullah currently deliver?+

Hibbullah currently serves customers in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

How much is the delivery fee?+

The current delivery fee is a flat ৳80 for the service area.

Which payment method is available?+

Orders are paid for with cash on delivery.

Can I follow my order after checkout?+

Yes. The app includes an order list, an order detail timeline, and in-app order updates.

Can I request a return?+

You can request a return for eligible order items from the order details. Return requests are reviewed by the Hibbullah team.

Is Hibbullah available on Android?+

Hibbullah is Android-first. Contact the team for the current app access and installation details.
